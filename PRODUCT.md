## Platform

web

## Users

Two buyers, weighted equally:

- **Founders who built their product with AI tools** (Lovable, Bolt and similar). The product works; it looks like every other AI-built app, and users say it looks generic. They want it fixed fast and cheaply, without hiring a designer full time.
- **Funded SaaS teams whose dashboard is the bottleneck.** Activation has plateaued, demos stall at the dashboard, or they are about to hire a product designer. They want one core screen redesigned properly and a kit their engineers can keep using.

Both arrive on the marketing site cold, usually from outreach or a link, and decide in one visit whether to ask for the free teardown.

## Product Purpose

Kyre redesigns the interfaces of products built in the AI era and ships the redesign as a pull request in the client's own code and stack. Success is a measurable change in the one number the redesign is judged on (sign-up or trial-to-paid conversion for apps; time-to-answer, demo-to-close or weekly active use for dashboards), not a set of mockups.

## Positioning

- Delivered as code in the client's repository, not as design files to hand to an engineer.
- Fixed scope and fixed price, agreed before work starts; nothing billed by the hour.
- Judged on one business number named up front.
- A free teardown first: one genuine strength, three fixable wins and a before/after of one screen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Next.js export on Vercel; no backend. The teardown form opens the visitor's email client.
- The site must stay smooth on low-power laptops without a GPU (a lighter tier exists, html[data-lite]).
- Market context quoted on the site (rescue builds $13,000–25,000+, agencies near $25,000) comes from a November 2026 scan of public pricing.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Kyre. No visual asset is fixed: the wordmark, accent colour and the hero scroll story may all change in a redesign.
- Voice: plain, specific, no hype; claims stated only when they can be checked.

## Product Principles

- Show the work, do not describe it: every claim about design quality is demonstrated on the page itself.
- One number first, on the site as in the redesigns it sells.
- Honest labelling over implied proof.
- Equal paths for the two buyers until one is clearly winning.
- Fast on every machine, including ones without a GPU.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA for text contrast, tap targets and keyboard use; a reduced-motion path for every animation.
