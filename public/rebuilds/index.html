How a rebuild ships

About a week for an app, about two for a dashboard, from your URL to a merged pull request.

  1. Day 0

    You send a URL

    We sign up the way your customers do and record the first session, start to finish.

  2. Day 1

    Teardown

    Every issue marked like the ones above, ranked by what it's likely costing you in signups or retention.

  3. Days 2–6

    Rebuild

    In your repo, on your stack. The screens that matter most go first.

  4. Day 7

    Ship

    Merged and deployed, with the one number the redesign is judged on.
