# Surface brief: trykyre.com home (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: an AI-built-app founder or a SaaS team lead, arriving cold from outreach, deciding in one visit whether to ask for the free teardown. Action: request the teardown (#teardown), second path book a call. Proof on hand: none from clients; every example labelled (Marlo fictional, sample teardown illustrative, Agnost unsolicited concept). Constraints: static export, 60fps on two cores with no GPU (html[data-lite]), WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced-motion path.

Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine). Replaces the incumbent world (light paper, hot orange, glass shatter), which is kept only as evidence; rollback in ROLLBACK.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A teardown, literally. The page is the bench where a product's screen is taken apart and put back together better. It refuses the studio-site default of a centred headline over a floating glossy screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: A self-healing cutting mat: deep mat green field (#1d5642 family) ruled with a pale grid, ruler numerals on the edges. Parts lie on it as physical objects with real offset shadows (flat, lifted, held). Numbered yellow sticker dots with hairline leaders label every part. Archivo (wide and narrow cuts of one family) speaks; Martian Mono only measures (part numbers, rulers, counts, prices). Yellow is the one action colour.

STORY: The visitor sees their kind of screen dismantled into parts, each labelled with what is wrong, then reassembled as the redesign, and understands that this is exactly what the free teardown gives them. They request it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left third: headline, one line of support, yellow "Get a free teardown" button. Right two thirds: the Marlo screen lying on the mat at a slight angle, ruler edge across the top. Scrolling pulls its parts apart onto the mat with numbered labels, then assembles the new screen in their place.

FORM: Teardown mat, position 1 on the ordered list (my pick card); seed:565e93ef3b633007.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
